What we heardIn your words

This is a healthy company that cannot see one part of itself.

We want to start by saying what is working, because a lot is. Referrals are steady. The phone rings daily. The team has been together for years with almost no turnover. Clients come back, and they say why in their reviews. Nothing in this document is a rescue plan.

Room to grow intoAround 25 projects a week is possible at current staffing, and the calendar is not consistently full. Growth here means filling capacity you already have.

Ten steps. Nine of them live or die on somebody remembering.

About the numbers in this document. Your team estimates roughly 5,000 contacts in Streak and roughly 1,500 on the Mailchimp list. We are using the word roughly on purpose. One of the first tasks in any path below is to count exactly, so that every number after that is one you can trust.

The ideaIn one sentence

Before spending a dollar reaching strangers, make the system you already own visible, connected, and working the contacts you already have.

Roughly 5,000 people already know Ideal exists. They sit in Streak today, mostly untouched after the job ends. The three paths below are in order of importance, on purpose. Each one stands alone, and each one makes the next one stronger.

2
Path twoThe system of record

Connect.

One place where every lead lands, every next step is obvious, and nothing depends on somebody remembering. There are two honest ways to get there, and we will build whichever one you choose.

Option A. Rebuild inside Streak.

  • One simple pipeline, built on the design Mercedes has already started. Her instinct is right: colorized, obvious next step, nothing extra.
  • Every door from the website feeds it automatically, through connector tools.
  • Existing boxes consolidated, duplicates flagged, nothing deleted.
  • Keeps the tool the team already knows, inside Gmail.
Honestly: faster and cheaper, and it fixes today's pain. The trade is that Streak stays a generic tool shaped by someone else. The sprawl that happened once can happen again, and the automations live in a third-party service with its own monthly fee.
Our recommendation

Option B. A system built for Ideal.

  • A small custom system shaped to your exact ten steps, and nothing else. If Ideal does not do it, the screen does not show it.
  • Your existing Streak contacts and leads come along, with source and history preserved. Nothing is deleted from Streak; it stays untouched until you choose to retire it.
  • New leads flow in from email and the website by themselves, source recorded, welcome sent, and placed on the right call list.
  • You own it outright. The code, the data, the documentation. No seat fees, no per-user pricing, no subscription to us required.
Honestly: it costs more up front and takes longer. We recommend it anyway, because the ten steps it needs to fit are unusually clear, and because owning the system is what makes every later idea in this document cheap instead of expensive.

3
Path threeFollow-up that runs itself

Engage.

Today, every offer goes to the same 1,500 or so people, and the team is rightly careful not to email them too often. The fix is not more email. It is the right email, to the right group, at the right moment, with a person approving every word before anything sends.

Three groups, three different conversations. A brand new lead gets a short, warm welcome series that stops the moment they reply or book. A just-finished client gets a thank-you and, at the right moment, a review invitation. A past client hears from you seasonally, like a neighbor, not a billboard. Nobody gets everything, so nobody gets tired of you.

And this is where the portal finally earns its keep

You are already paying for a library of marketing formulas through the Business Master Class portal. Today it is a library: full of good material, connected to nothing. In this path it becomes a supply line.

1. The portal

A formula is chosen, for example from the Referral System Builder. We copy it into your own library first, so nothing you run ever depends on the portal being up or a membership being active.

2. The draft

AI drafts the sequence in Ideal's voice, calm and warm, using the formula as its brief. Drafting is all it does.

3. Your approval

A person reads every word and approves it, or edits it, or throws it out. Nothing sends without this step. Ever.

4. Sent and counted

The approved sequence runs by itself and reports back in plain numbers: delivered, opened, replied, booked.

Four things we noticed and set aside, on purpose.

Each of these is real, none of them is urgent, and none of them is included in the numbers at the end of this page. We would rather name them now than surprise you with them later.

Google and the neighborhood

Your website does not currently tell Google that Ideal is a local business, and some older directory listings still carry a previous company name. Cleaning this up is inexpensive and helps the people already searching for you. A natural follow-on to Path One.

A review engine

Your happiest clients say wonderful things in person. A simple, well-timed invitation at project close would put more of that in writing where new clients can find it. Mercedes suggested this on our call, and she is right.

The Drive

Seventeen years of files deserve their own conversation. We would want to look at the Drive together before putting any shape or number on that work, so it is deliberately not part of this blueprint.

Paid advertising, eventually

You asked whether there is a bigger way to reach people who do not know you yet. There is, and the honest order is: measure first, connect second, and then paid reach becomes worth buying, because you can finally see what it returns.

Our commitmentsWhat we will not touch

Some things are yours, and stay that way.

The first call

Tria's first conversation with a new client, and Michael's relationships with senior clients, are the product. No system we build will ever sit between you and that call.

Client documents

Your clients trust you with insurance papers, powers of attorney, and estate documents. Nothing about client documents moves anywhere until you have set the boundaries with us, in writing, first.

The team's work

Nothing anyone has built gets deleted or overwritten. Existing pipelines and lists are preserved and carried across, and the people who made them are part of designing what comes next.

Every outgoing word

AI drafts. People approve. Nothing is ever sent to a client or a lead without a human deciding it should be. This is not a setting we might change later. It is how we build.

How we workSo this sounds like us in six months too

We build it, we teach it, and then you should not need us.

Something we said on the call, and want in writing: we do not want anything sticky, where you cannot operate without us. If we were hit by a bus tomorrow, someone on your team could step in, because the system is documented, the team is trained, and you own all of it.

You own everything

The system, the data, the documentation, and the how-it-works guides are yours from day one. There is no version of this where you are renting your own business back from us.

Training is part of the build

We do not hand over a tool and walk away. We work alongside Mercedes and the team until it is genuinely adopted, and we would rather build one thing that gets used than five that collect dust.

Built for the people using it

Anything that requires learning a complicated screen has already failed. The measure of this system is that it gives Tria fewer places to look, not more.

We will tell you no

Part of what you are paying for is a company that says so when a plan is wrong, including ours. If a path here stops making sense once we are inside the work, we will say that plainly.
