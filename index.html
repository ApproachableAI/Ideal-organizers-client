The planWhat we are proposing, plainly

Three projects. This is the whole plan.

First, what this is not: a rescue. Referrals are steady, the phone rings daily, and the team has been together for years. This is a healthy company that cannot see one part of itself, with room for roughly 25 projects a week that the calendar does not consistently fill. Growth here means filling capacity you already have, and these three projects, in this order, do exactly that.

1

Fix the CRM

One home for every client and lead.

  • Three ways to get there: clean up the Streak you have, build a system that is yours alone, or have us set up a ready-made platform for you. You pick, we build.
  • Every way into the business feeds it automatically, with the source recorded.
  • Your existing contacts come along. Nothing anyone built gets deleted.
2

Turn on the outreach

Your client list, hearing from you again.

  • Automated email drips and campaigns, up to six to start, running from the CRM.
  • Built from the Darren Hardy MVP A.I. Portal formulas you already pay for.
  • A context file library of those scripts, organized and shared with the whole team. A person approves every word before anything sends.
3

Get the insights

Know exactly what is working.

  • All the data tied together in one place.
  • A Monday scorecard that lands in your inbox by itself.
  • Every lead counted with the door it came through: website, phone, Angi, referral. Marketing decisions stop being guesses.

Each project stands on its own. Every cost lives in one place, under Pricing, so the numbers stay side by side.

Project oneFix the CRM

One home for everything.

One place where every lead lands and every next step is obvious. There are three honest ways to get it, and we will build whichever one you choose.

Option A. Clean up Streak.

  • One simple pipeline, built on the design Mercedes has already started. Her instinct is right: colorized, obvious next step, nothing extra.
  • The website and email doors connected through connector tools. Duplicates flagged, nothing deleted.
Honestly: the lightest lift, and it fixes today's pain. The trade is that Streak stays a generic tool shaped by someone else, and the sprawl that happened once can happen again.
Our recommendation

Option B. Build your own.

  • A small custom system shaped to your exact ten steps, and nothing else. If Ideal does not do it, the screen does not show it.
  • Contacts, history, and source migrated. Streak stays untouched as the fallback until you retire it.
  • You own it outright: the code, the data, the documentation. No seat fees, no subscription to us.
Honestly: the longest to arrive. We recommend it anyway, because owning the system is what makes every later idea in this document easy to add instead of expensive to buy.

Option C. A ready-made platform, set up by us.

  • A proven client-management platform, configured completely by us to Ideal's ten steps and carrying Ideal's branding. Your team never touches the setup.
  • The broadest toolbox of the three from day one. Contacts migrated and team trained, same as Option B.
Honestly: the broadest feature set soonest, but you rent it rather than own it, and part of our setup work is hiding everything Ideal does not need. That is the same crowding that happened with Streak, held back by configuration instead of ownership.

Project twoTurn on the outreach

Your client list, hearing from you again.

Roughly 5,000 contacts sit in Streak today, by your team's estimate, and about 1,500 of them are on the Mailchimp list. Most never hear from you after the job ends, and today every offer goes to the same list at the same pace. This project puts the system from Project 1 to work on the people who already know you: current clients, past clients, and the ones who never booked each hear something different, at a pace that fits.

Where the Darren Hardy MVP A.I. Portal earns its keep

You are already paying for a library of marketing formulas through the Business Master Class portal. Today it is a library: full of good material, connected to nothing. In this project it becomes a supply line.

1. The portal

A formula is chosen, for example from the Referral System Builder. We copy it into your own library first, so nothing you run ever depends on the portal being up or a membership being active.

2. The draft

AI drafts the campaign in Ideal's voice, calm and warm, using the formula as its brief. Drafting is all it does.

3. Your approval

A person reads every word and approves it, or edits it, or throws it out. Nothing sends without this step. Ever.

4. Sent and counted

The approved campaign runs by itself from the CRM and reports back in plain numbers: delivered, opened, replied, booked.

Up to six campaigns and drips to start, chosen with you. For example:

Welcome a new leadUnsigned agreement nudgeReview invitation at closeSeasonal past-client check-inClub member renewalsReferral thank-you

The Automations tab in the sample above is exactly where these live. What you clicked through is the shape of the real thing, and every sequence stops itself the moment a person replies or books.

The context file library, for the whole company

Every formula and script we use comes out of the portal and into a library that belongs to Ideal: organized by job, written in your voice, and open to the whole team rather than one login. Because the library is yours, the campaigns keep running even in months when the portal membership is paused.

And once the counting works, four doors open.

Each of these is real, none of them is urgent, and none of them is in the numbers below. We would rather name them now than surprise you with them later.

Google and the neighborhood

Your website does not currently tell Google that Ideal is a local business, and some older directory listings still carry a previous company name. Cleaning this up, and the website's search standing with it, is inexpensive and helps the people already searching for you.

A review engine

Your happiest clients say wonderful things in person. A simple, well-timed invitation at project close would put more of that in writing where new clients can find it. Mercedes suggested this on our call, and she is right.

Paid advertising, finally provable

You asked whether there is a bigger way to reach people who do not know you yet. There is, and the honest order is: connect first, measure second, and then paid reach becomes worth buying, because you can finally see what it returns.

Our commitmentsHow we work, and what we will not touch

Something we said on the call, and want in writing: we do not want anything sticky, where you cannot operate without us. If we were hit by a bus tomorrow, someone on your team could step in, because the system is documented, the team is trained, and you own all of it.

The first call is yours

Tria's first conversation with a new client, and Michael's relationships with senior clients, are the product. No system we build will ever sit between you and that call.

Client documents

Your clients trust you with insurance papers, powers of attorney, and estate documents. Nothing about client documents moves anywhere until you have set the boundaries with us, in writing, first.

The team's work
